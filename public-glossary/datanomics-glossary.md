# Datanomics Public Glossary

**Datanomics**  
The economic science of how data and knowledge create, transfer, and
accumulate value. Datanomics draws from platform economics, information
economics, communication theory, and the study of data as a production
factor and exchangeable service.

**Data Sharing Ecosystem**  
A system comprising a community of agents, a shared data resource, and
an analytic system, connected across three layers: Asset Layer (data and
system), Management Layer (community organisation and value allocation),
and Output Layer (valuable interaction).

**Extraction Value**  
The value realised from a single use or transaction of a data or knowledge
asset. Treats data as a private, rivalrous good.

**Extensibility Value**  
The value created when a data or knowledge asset is reused, recombined,
and compounded across multiple applications and stakeholders. Treats data
through a service-dominant logic.

**Information Chain Failure**  
A vertical form of market failure occurring in data supply chains when
the conditions for value creation from data are not met at one or more
stages of the chain.

**Knowledge Impact Efficiency**  
The proportion of potential knowledge value that is successfully
commercialised through an organisation's knowledge production process.
Best-in-breed: 75-85%. Observed range in diagnostics: 41-62%.

**Parallel Payoff**  
The simultaneous realisation of multiple value streams from a single
data or knowledge investment: internal use, external sale, and operational
improvement.
